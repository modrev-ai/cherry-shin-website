Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 August 2026

cherrystudio.art (“the site”) shows recent public posts by Cherry Shin, gathered from her own social media accounts. This policy explains what data the site handles.

The site has no accounts and no sign-in. It does not ask visitors for any personal information, and it does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

What we collect from visitors

  • Nothing you type. There are no forms, sign-ups or comments.
  • No advertising or analytics cookies. The site sets none.
  • One local preference. Whether you have chosen to hide wide videos is stored in your own browser using localStorage. It never leaves your device and is not readable by us. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Standard server logs. Our host (Vercel) records ordinary request data such as IP address, timestamp and user agent, for security and reliability. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Data we retrieve from social platforms

The site uses the official APIs of YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, authorised by the account holder, to retrieve her own public posts: titles and captions, thumbnail images, publish dates, engagement counts, and links back to the original post. It also retrieves her public follower and post totals, and her public profile picture, which is shown at the top of the page.

This covers only the account holder's own content. The site does not retrieve, store or process data about other platform users, their posts, their comments, or their profiles.

TikTok and X are not currently connected. No data is requested from them. Cards for a platform that is not connected show placeholder content, labelled Sample on the card, which carries no engagement figures and no date. Those cards are not the account holder's posts. Should either platform be connected later, it would be on the same basis as the others above.

How that data is handled

  • Responses are cached briefly on the server — around ten minutes — so the site does not call the platforms once per visitor. The cache holds only the public post data described above.
  • Nothing is written to a database. There is no persistent store of platform data.
  • Access credentials are held as server-side environment variables. They are never sent to your browser and are not present in the site's source code.

Embedded content

Some posts play through an embedded player supplied by YouTube, Facebook or TikTok. When a player loads, that platform may set its own cookies and receive your IP address and user agent, under its own privacy policy rather than this one: Google/YouTube, Meta, TikTok. Posts that are not embedded show a still image and a link instead, which loads nothing from the platform until you click it.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share visitor data. There is essentially none to share.

Your choices

You can clear the stored display preference at any time through your browser's site-data settings. Because the site holds no account and no personal record of you, there is nothing else to request or delete — but if you believe otherwise, contact us below.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above reflects the current version.
