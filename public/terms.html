What the site is

The site is a personal showcase. It displays posts published by the account holder on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, retrieved through those platforms' official APIs using credentials granted by the account holder. A platform that is not connected is represented by placeholder cards, marked Sample and shown without engagement figures or dates.
